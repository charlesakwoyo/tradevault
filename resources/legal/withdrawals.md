## Before you request a withdrawal
- Your identity verification (KYC) must be approved.
- Withdrawals are sent only to accounts or phone numbers in your own name.
- The minimum and maximum amounts and any fees are shown on the withdrawal form **before** you submit.

## Processing
Requests move through: Pending → Under review → Approved → Processing → Completed. The current status and, where a request cannot proceed, the reason are always shown in your account.

## Funds while pending
The requested amount is moved from your available balance to your reserved balance. If a request is rejected or cancelled, the full amount is returned to your available balance.
